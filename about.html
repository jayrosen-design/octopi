Curiosity, made tangible

Research you can explore.

Octopi AI is an interactive prototype that gives research agents a playful, physical presence. Each clay octopus represents a perspective; shared bubbles connect ideas and retain their origins.

How the experience fits together

  1. Welcome introduces the crew, then Account creates a local account or signs you in with Google or as a Firebase guest.
  2. Home opens directly into the interactive reef: foreground research agents with their own data bubble, background Garden pods, and shared knowledge bubbles in one scene.
  3. Ask a question on Home and the research desk opens as an overlay in the ocean: plan, search, review and report on the left, the octopi chat on the right.
  4. Each agent keeps its own collection (plans, sources, reports, review notes) and can contribute it to the crew's bubble. Projects gathers everything as one project file for export, import or Google Drive sharing.
  5. Full Garden remains available for extended exploration, moving collaborators between pods, or swimming as an octopus.
  6. Mold an Agent lets you sculpt and personalize an octopus, then save it to your local crew.

What is live, and what is a demonstration?

Garden conversations and graph relationships are illustrative. Moving between pods creates authored introductions. Graph positions do not represent a connected vector database. Reference links let you inspect the original sources.

Live research uses your connected NaviGator and Tavily services. Kokoro speech uses a NaviGator key. Keys entered in the interface stay in page memory; a local server can also use environment-provided keys.

Your clay, your device

Saved agents, research, agent collections, garden bubbles and your account are stored in this browser. Export them as a project file, or sync them to a shared Google Drive folder from the Projects page. Provider keys are kept in tab memory unless you opt in to remembering them on the device. Garden placements currently last for the page session. Clay deformation and pickup use artistic spring and gravity approximations. The garden and sculpting scenes use WebGPU when available, with a supported renderer fallback.

A thoughtful starting point

AI-generated reports can contain mistakes. Open original sources, check citations, and apply your judgment before using a result.
